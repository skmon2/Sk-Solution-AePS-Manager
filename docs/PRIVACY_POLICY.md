1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes privacy and data-protection considerations for users of Sk Solution AePS Manager, a Windows application for digital transaction and record management.

Depending on the features you use, you may enter or manage transaction records, amounts, service details, and other business information within the application.

Please do not enter information that is unnecessary for your work.

The storage and processing of information depend on the application's implementation and configuration. Before relying on this policy, the software maintainer should verify where application data is stored, whether any information is transmitted to external services, and how backups are handled.

4. Third-Party Services

The application may rely on third-party services or integrations for certain features. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the applicable terms before providing information to a third-party service.

5. Security

Users should protect their devices, keep software updated, use strong account credentials where applicable, and store backups securely. No software or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

Do not share passwords, OTPs, API keys, customer information, or confidential transaction records in public forums or GitHub issues.

The software maintainer should document any actual collection, sharing, or disclosure of user information before publishing a final privacy commitment.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data retention and deletion depend on the application's storage design and the user's configuration. Users should confirm where their records are stored and maintain appropriate backups before deleting application data.

8. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for business and transaction-record management, not for children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as the application and its data practices change. The date above should be updated when a revised policy is published.
